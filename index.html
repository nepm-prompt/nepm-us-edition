2. Phase 1 Prompt (Master Sheet)

Workflow Steps:
  1. Copy the generated prompt below and paste it into ChatGPT, Claude, or Gemini (Keep Phase 1 and Phase 2 in the same chat session).
  2. Review the AI-generated Master Sheet carefully. Request any revisions directly in the chat.
  3. Once satisfied, type APPROVE to finalize the Master Sheet. (Note: Do not generate the Student Sheet yet).
